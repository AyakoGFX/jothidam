1. Fundamental Principle: The Mind and the Moon

Everyone experiences days that are exceptionally peaceful and days filled with unexpected tension. In Vedic astrology, daily happiness, peace, and satisfaction originate entirely from the state of the human mind.

  • The Moon as the Ruler: The Moon is the Manokaraka (the celestial factor controlling the mind).
  • The Transit Timeline: Unlike slower planets, the Moon travels rapidly through the 12 zodiac signs, staying in a single sign for approximately 2¼ days (54 hours).
  • Gochara Strength: Daily predictions are not calculated from your birth ascendant (Lagna), but rather from your Moon sign (Rasi). If the transiting Moon is in an auspicious position relative to your birth Rasi, your day will be successful; if it is in a malefic position, obstacles will arise.

2. The 12 House Transits of the Moon

The impact of the Moon shifts dramatically depending on which house it is transiting relative to your birth sign. In the original teaching, Auspicious Frequencies are indicated as favorable, and Malefic Frequencies are indicated as unfavorable.

2.1. Favorable Transits (Ishtasthanam)

When the Moon passes through the 1st, 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th, and 11th houses, it brings positive results:

  • 1st House (Janma Rasi): Favorable. Delivers satisfying food, peaceful sleep, financial profits, and the desire to purchase new clothes or gifts for loved ones. It brings general domestic peace and maternal happiness.
  • 3rd House: Favorable. Brings unexpected monetary gains, support or happiness from women, career promotions, and sudden profits in business.
  • 6th House: Favorable. Promotes excellent physical health, mental bliss, financial growth, and the systematic destruction or weakening of your enemies' influence.
  • 7th House: Favorable. Guarantees satisfying meals, deep sleep, supplemental income, and a high level of social respect and public value.
  • 10th House: Favorable. Bestows "royal respect" (high recognition), significant developments or promotions at work, professional praise, physical vitality, and mental contentment.
  • 11th House: Favorable. The house of pure gain. Relatives and friends bring gifts or business opportunities, loans are easily approved, and career ambitions are successfully fulfilled.

2.2. Unfavorable Transits (Vedasthanam / Malefic Places)

When the Moon passes through the 2nd, 4th, 5th, 8th, 9th, and 12th houses, it triggers vulnerabilities:

  • 2nd House: Malefic. Causes Karmic Vignam (obstacles in daily operations). Easy tasks become tedious, financial losses or home thefts can occur, and minor dishonor or reputational damage may manifest.
  • 4th House: Malefic. Induces a constant fear of losing one's job or status. Prone to water-borne ailments (severe colds/throat issues) and unnecessary domestic lawsuits or relative disputes.
  • 5th House: Malefic. Manifests as physical lethargy or arthritis-like discomfort (numbness in hands and feet), alongside heavy mental anxiety, sorrow, and operational delays.
  • 8th House (Chandrashtama): Highly Malefic. Triggers intense mental confusion, poor decision-making capacity (feeling like "a fading lamp in a wild forest"), a severe risk of food poisoning or stomach distress, and sudden, intense anger that leads to unnecessary fights.
  • 9th House: Malefic. Completely drains personal courage and self-confidence. Hesitation dominates decision-making, unexpected stomach aches occur, and progress stalls.
  • 12th House: Malefic. Triggers profound laziness, absolute loss of interest in work, eating, or studying, and severe mental depression. Monies promised by debtors are delayed, and business encounters stagnation.

4. The Two Absolute Modifiers: Vedasthanam and Ashtakavarga

To understand why generic television or newspaper predictions do not always come true for you, you must apply two deep Shastric rules that modify baseline Gochara transits:

4.1. 1. The Rule of Vedasthanam (Obstruction)

Even if the Moon is transiting a highly auspicious house (1, 3, 6, 7, 10, or 11), its positive effects can be completely blocked if another planet occupies its corresponding Veda sign at the same time. Think of it like placing a massive wooden board in front of a bright torch—the light exists, but it cannot reach you. Conversely, if the Moon is in a malefic house but faces a Vedham obstruction, the negative effects are thankfully minimized.

4.2. 2. The Rule of Ashtakavarga Bindus (Personalized Score)

Every individual horoscope contains a personalized Ashtakavarga chart mapping out strength points (Bindus) for each zodiac sign.

  • More than 4 Bindus (>4): If the Moon transits a sign where your birth chart holds more than 4 points, malefic transits (like the 12th house or Chandrashtama) lose their destructive power, and auspicious transits yield maximum rewards.
  • Less than 4 Bindus (<4): If a sign has only 2 or 3 points, even a standard "favorable" transit will fail to produce good results, leading to astrological confusion if calculated superficially.
